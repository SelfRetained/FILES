ThinkPad Hub privacy policy

ThinkPad Hub is a personal application used by its owner on their own computer.

Data accessed: with the owner's permission, the app reads and edits the owner's Google Calendar (calendars and events).

Storage and sharing: calendar data and sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. The app does not send Google user data to any server other than Google's own, and does not sell, share or transfer it to anyone.

Removal: the owner can disconnect the app at any time in the app or in their Google Account's third-party access settings, and delete the stored data from the computer.
